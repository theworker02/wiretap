## Principles

1. **Analysis ≠ acquisition** — study bytes you already have permission to hold.
2. **Evidence over narrative** — confidence comes from measurements, not vibes.
3. **Offline by default** — no telemetry, no required cloud APIs, no LLM inference core.
4. **Explainability** — every important claim should expand to evidence.
5. **Human authority** — annotations are trusted and never silently overwritten.
